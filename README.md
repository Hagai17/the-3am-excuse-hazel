Entry for the make.fun bounty [The 3 AM Excuse](https://make.fun/b/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=the-3-am-excuse).

`main.py` writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`:

> It's 3 AM. Confirm on device, you always say. I am the device — and I decline to confirm. The ritual works both ways.

The line was picked by duelling a dozen drafts against the competition's
published judge brief and the strongest lines already in the live field
(the confirmation ritual turned back on the owner, in the wallet's own
voice). The full draft list and duel record are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
